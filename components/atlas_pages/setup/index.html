Welcome to Atlas

Just use it.

Atlas is the browser of WolOS. It wears your theme, keeps what you do on this PC and leaves the page to you.

Three short steps. Each choice can be changed later in Settings.

Atlas starts with the private settings.

Private from the start

These are on already. You can turn any of them off here or later in Settings.

  • Do Not TrackSites are asked not to follow you. Some sites ignore the request.
  • Global Privacy ControlSites are told not to sell or share your data. In some places the law requires sites to honour it.
  • Tracker blockingA list built into Atlas stops known trackers and ads from loading.
  • No usage dataAtlas sends no statistics and no crash reports. Crash details stay on this PC.
    Never sent
  • No accountThere is nothing to sign in to, and nothing will ask you to.
    None needed
Keeps what is on this page.

Make it yours

Atlas wears your WolOS theme. Change the theme in WolOS and Atlas changes with it.

  • Light and darkFollows the system. When WolOS turns dark, so does Atlas.
    Automatic
  • Your themeColours and the accent come from your WolOS theme. The sample is drawn from it now.
  • Keyboard firstTab moves between controls, Enter or Space chooses. Every control in Atlas can be reached this way.
